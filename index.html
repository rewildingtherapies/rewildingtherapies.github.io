The Toolkit

Free tools for time outside. Take what you need.

Small, simple tools for noticing the land, listening to your dreams and reading the sky. They work on any phone or computer, and your entries stay on your device.

Sit Spot Journal Same place, different day. Log what you notice, track your visitors, and watch the year turn. Includes the Four Shields. Open Dream Journal Capture dreams on waking, reflect later, and see which symbols keep returning. With a short guide to Jungian dream work. Open Weather Wise Check the forecast for your spot and get a clear go or no-go, with tips for every kind of weather and neurodivergent awareness. Open
Prefer pen and paper?

Printable and fillable versions of the journals, ready for A4, iPad or reMarkable.
